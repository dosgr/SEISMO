The experiment

SEISMO is a free, open-source science game and educational experiment. Real readings from seismographs in Greece and around the world supply the input. When the time window is fully covered, a public calculation selects one of the wheel’s 37 numbers. Virtual chips have no monetary value. There are no deposits, withdrawals, cash bets or prizes.

FAQ and science notes

How the idea began

The idea grew in Greece, a country with frequent seismic activity, from a desire to understand earthquakes better. Everyday ground vibrations inspired an experience that brings together seismology, technology, mathematics, education and play.

Why does Earth vibrate?

Ground motion is recorded almost continuously. Ocean waves, weather, human activity and earthquakes can all leave signals. Ocean-generated microseisms are part of the broader ambient seismic noise. A seismometer senses motion and the recorder turns it into timestamped readings.

Where does the data come from?

Your browser requests actual miniSEED via HTTPS/FDSN from public services including the National Observatory of Athens, GEOFON, Caltech/SCEDC and EarthScope. The catalog defines stations, routes and backup priority. Requests originate from your device, so providers see your connection’s IP address. Greek priority refers to the sensor’s physical location. EarthScope relays Greek HL/HT channels and EPOS France provides CL streams from the Gulf of Corinth. SCEDC, international EarthScope routes, and GEOFON are backups in their documented round modes.

How is the number calculated?

SPIN locks a future UTC window. The first three complete physical stations in the published order supply their integer samples to SHA-256. SHA-256 produces a digital fingerprint. Rejection sampling assigns six byte values to each number and avoids simple modulo bias under the uniform-byte assumption. At the cutoff the published order determines selection and duplicate routes to one physical instrument are removed. Response time does not determine priority.

How can I verify a round?

Save TXT for reading and JSON for verification. Use embedded miniSEED where redistribution is allowed. For other routes, obtain the provider’s original file, add it to the verifier and compare fingerprints. With the files already available, the standalone edition can recompute the number offline.

Why is there a 330-second option?

The extended round also allows time for slower international GEOFON routes. NOA and NOA2 remain in the catalog for archival checks. Duration and the window are committed when you press SPIN.

What does NO RESULT mean?

Fewer than three stations delivered complete valid samples before the cutoff. The round log shows coverage, arrival times and failures. Latency depends on the station, the provider and the network path. Failed rounds remain visible in session history.

Scientific limits

Correlated samples and overlapping windows limit what can be inferred about independence and unpredictability. SEISMO is an experiment and has no random-number generator or gambling system certification. It does not predict earthquakes.

Knowledge, preparedness and respect

We cannot stop earthquakes. We can study them, understand them and learn to live with them through knowledge, preparedness and respect for nature.
